About

Quantum Computing Fall Fest 2026 gathers researchers, engineers, students, and industry leaders for five days of deep technical talks, hands-on workshops, hackathons, poster sessions, and networking. Whether you are new to quantum computing or working on cutting-edge algorithms, there's content for all levels.

  • 5 days of sessions: Keynotes, tutorials, workshops, lightning talks
  • Hands-on labs and a 48-hour hackathon
  • Hybrid attendance — in-person venue + livestream
  • Student poster session and industry showcase
